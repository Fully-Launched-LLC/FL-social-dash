## The 3-3-3: pillars, formats, perspectives

### What this document is

Every client gets a **3-3-3**: **3 pillars** (what we always talk about), **3 formats** (the shapes our videos take) and **3 unique perspectives** (where we see things differently from everyone else). These are the categories we stay in. Every content idea should trace back to one pillar, one format and one perspective. **[Tait]**

**Fully Launched is the exception: 4 pillars instead of 3**, one for each service. **[Tait]**

**1. Websites**
- **What we do:** redesign business websites to bring in new customers. **[Tait]**
- **Our angle:** custom websites that actually drive results and don't just look cool. Anyone can design a website with AI now; our strategy is making it simple, a simple, easy-to-use website. **[Tait]**

- **Says what he's learned, in first person.** *"I’ve learned that"* opens a line in two different posts.

Never write "It's not X, it's Y" or "this isn't about X, it's about Y." That negation-then-correction is the clearest tell a machine wrote it. Just say the thing.

Tait's own posts do use a milder version ("The goal is to help people…not build a social media that looks aesthetic."). When writing for him, say the first half and stop: "The goal is to help people."

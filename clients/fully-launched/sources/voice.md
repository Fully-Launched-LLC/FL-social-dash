## How I sound

- **One line, then a line break.** Almost every sentence is its own paragraph. Posts are 6 to 15 short lines. *"It’ll turn around and bite you in the face."*
- **The first line is the hook, and it stands alone.** A question, a number, a rule, or a quote he then argues with. *"< 2 hours per week."* · *"Never grab the tail of a wiener dog."* · *"Do you ever just stare at your phone trying to think of an idea for a LinkedIn post?"*
- **A small personal story first, then the lesson, then the business.** The wiener dog at age 5 turns into two life lessons and then "reach out to us."
- **Says what he's learned, in first person.** *"I’ve learned that"* opens a line in two different posts.
- **Ellipses for the pause and the punchline.** *"The dog’s name was Stroker, too…I totally should have seen it coming."* He knows it: *"I just noticed recently that I’ve been using a lot of ellipses."*
- **Lists with no bullets or numbers,** one item per line. *"Go on a drive / Workout / Do a hobby"*
- **Funny, and happy to laugh at himself.** *"as I’m using AI for the cover photo. Anyways, just a funny observation."*
- **Casual.** Sometimes all lowercase (post 1), the odd typo, contractions everywhere. Ends on a plain takeaway or one ask.

## The one pattern to kill

Never write "It's not X, it's Y" or "this isn't about X, it's about Y." That negation-then-correction is the clearest tell a machine wrote it. Just say the thing.

Tait's own posts do use a milder version ("The goal is to help people…not build a social media that looks aesthetic."). When writing for him, say the first half and stop: "The goal is to help people."

## Matteo's voice

From one voice memo (`2026-09-30-matteo-voice-memo.md`), answering the customer questions. Every phrase is quoted exactly. One recording is not much, so treat this as a first read.

**How he sounds**

- **Short line to land it.** *"That felt fixable."* · *"Usually, it's conversion."*

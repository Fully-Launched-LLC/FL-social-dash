## Post 2: staring at your phone

Do you ever just stare at your phone trying to think of an idea for a LinkedIn post?

And the more you think, the less ideas you have.

I experience this all the time.

I’ve learned that I need to escape the rational mind.

When I’m in my irrational, intuitive mind, my mind wanders and connects ideas that have never been connected before.

Do an activity that blocks your conscious mind:

Go on a drive
Workout
Do a hobby

The key then is to make sure you write down your ideas. They come and go fast!

## Post 7: the #1 way business owners fail

The #1 way business owners fail to bring in customers with content…

You are posting content that brings in the same people as you.

If you run a marketing company and you’re posting videos on how to build a marketing company from the ground up, you’re not going to attract businesses who need marketing services.

But if you’re selling a course/product to marketing companies than posting videos on how you built your marketing company is a great way to attract customers.

The goal is to help people…not build a social media that looks aesthetic.

Post content that solves problems.

Your viewers should have clear action steps after reading/watching your content.

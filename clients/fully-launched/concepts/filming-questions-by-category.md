# Fully Launched: Filming Questions by Category

Every Fully Launched video, sorted into our four categories: AI integration and systems, social media, websites and e-commerce. Each of us can film our own category in about an hour. Send each person their section.

**How to film a section:** set up the camera, have someone read each question from behind it, and answer in your own words, 30 seconds to 2 minutes each. The talking points under each idea are there if you want a nudge. Ideas marked **Needs from you** need a detail first.

**Post dates** are from the content calendar on the dashboard, where every video now starts with its category. Film by is 14 days before. Documentation videos (Building Fully Launched, IDs starting with B) sit in the category they're about.

Week 1 Feed Content Plan
Making GradGig visible, useful and familiar to busy Wheaton families.
4 FEED POSTS

CORE IDEA
Start with the customer’s life. Let GradGig become the useful answer.
Week 1 moves from relatability → humour → service discovery → a real human explanation from Luke.

WEEK 1
The creative system
Four posts, four different jobs — all aimed at making GradGig easier to recognise and understand.
01  RELATABILITY
Show the weekend problem before selling the solution.
02  HUMOUR
Make the pain recognisable enough to share.
03  DISCOVERY
Show how broad GradGig actually is from the customer perspective.
04  TRUST
Put Luke on camera so the brand feels local and human.
PRODUCTION RULE
Stock/AI creates the situation. Real GradGig content creates the proof.
The first three posts can be produced without waiting for new client footage. Luke’s Reel is the main Week 1 asset request.

POST 01
Your Saturday To-Do List
A supposedly free Saturday disappears under household jobs. GradGig appears only after the problem feels familiar.
REEL
STOCK / AI
RELATABILITY

01

02
Mow the lawn.
03
Clean the garage.
04
Move that thing that has been sitting there for 3 weeks.
05
So much for Saturday.
06
Your weekend does not have to become your to-do list.
STORY / STRUCTURE
• Open on a calm coffee moment: “You finally made it to Saturday.”
• Build the list: lawn → garage → moving something → one more annoying task.
• Break the repetition with: “Move that thing that has been sitting there for 3 weeks.”
• Return to the person/coffee: “So much for Saturday.”
• End card: “Your weekend doesn’t have to become your to-do list. Post the gig. Get an extra pair of hands.”
HOOK
You finally made it to Saturday.
PRODUCTION
12–15 sec · quick cuts · natural smartphone-style footage · light/comedic audio
ASSET DEPENDENCY
NONE — Createpossible can produce independently.
CAPTION DIRECTION
Saturday was supposed to be the day you slowed down. Some things need doing; that does not mean you have to do all of them yourself.

STORY / STRUCTURE
• Use one recognisable problem, ideally a chaotic garage.

PRODUCTION
8–12 sec · one or two clips · very light edit · dry humour
ASSET DEPENDENCY
NONE — one stock/AI clip can be enough.
CAPTION DIRECTION
You said this weekend. Then last weekend. Some jobs do not need another spot on next week’s list.

STORY / STRUCTURE
• 01 Cover: “There’s probably more you can get help with than you think.”
• 02 Around the house: yardwork, landscaping, painting, moving help.
• 03 The kids: tutoring, sports coaching, babysitting.
• 04 Tech: tech setup, coding, social media.
• 05 Pets: dog walking, pet care.
• 06 Skills you might not expect: photography, artwork, music lessons, personal training.
• 07 CTA: “What’s on your list? There might already be a local college student who can help.”
VISUAL RHYTHM
Alternate orange graphic slides, full-image slides, off-white layouts and beige panels.
PRODUCTION
Use GradGig branding + stock imagery. Avoid making every slide feel like the same Canva template.
ASSET DEPENDENCY
LOW — categories are based on the existing GradGig gig list supplied.
CAPTION DIRECTION
Sometimes it is not that you cannot do something. You just do not have the time — or someone else already has the skill you need.


POST 04
Luke: What Can I Actually Post on GradGig?
Luke answers a simple customer question and gives a few very different examples so GradGig feels broad, useful and human.
REEL
LUKE
TRUST

01
Luke: “People ask us what kind of jobs they can actually post…”
02
YARDWORK
03
TUTORING
04
PET CARE
05
TECH HELP
06
If you could use an extra pair of hands, post it.
STORY / STRUCTURE
• Luke opens directly to camera with the customer question.
• Give 3–5 deliberately different examples: yardwork, moving help, tutoring, pet care, tech.
• Key message: the job does not have to be huge.
• Edit with quick supporting B-roll and orange category labels.
• Close: “If there’s something you could use an extra pair of hands with, post it.”
FILMING PROMPT
In 20–30 seconds, answer: “What kinds of things can someone post on GradGig?” Give 3–5 very different examples.
PRODUCTION
Vertical phone video · natural light · conversational delivery · supporting B-roll added in edit
ASSET DEPENDENCY
HIGH — 20–30 sec vertical video from Luke.
CAPTION DIRECTION
Use Luke’s answer as the core of the caption, then reinforce the breadth of gigs and direct people to post what they need help with.

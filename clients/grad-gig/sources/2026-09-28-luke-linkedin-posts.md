## Post 3

5 things Im doing to stay ahead with AI

I’m reading read some good books written a while ago.

Anywhere from 5 years ago to centuries ago.

And of course... they don’t have anything about AI in them.

But AI is such a culture-shifting phenomena that it seems like all these books should be re-written to include AI.

It still seems like the wild, wild west.

Nobody knows what they are doing with AI or quite how much it will change the world.

Here's how I'm staying ahead:

- Learning new skills (vibe coding)
- Networking
- Reading real books (yes, the paper kind)
- Tracking with the new AI tools as they progress
- Praying
- Working as hard as I can
- Focusing on what matters most: faith and family!

## Post 4

You want what you can't have. 

Young people want to be old.
Old people want to be young.

Entrepreneurs envy the stability of a paycheck.
9-5ers crave the freedom of running something.

I've felt this from both sides working part time and building ventures on the side like Fully Launched.

For me, stable income isn't the enemy of the entrepreneurial life, it's what's currently funding it.

Sometimes that feels like a compromise, but it has also been the thing that lets me build without starving the business for cash before it's ready.

The trick isn't picking a side.

It's noticing when you're chasing the other side just because you don't have it, not because you actually need it.

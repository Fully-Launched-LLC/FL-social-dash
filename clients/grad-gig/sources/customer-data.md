## How to read this

- Every quote is copied **word for word** from the recordings. Nothing is invented and nothing is summarized.
- Words in **[square brackets]** are only there to make a transcript readable (a missing word, or the phone mishearing a word, like "invent" for vet). Everything outside the brackets is exactly what was said. **"..."** means words were left out of the middle.
- **Who we're talking to:** people who hire, not students. Tait: "this marketing and social media should be going towards parents and people who need to hire a trustworthy student." Luke describes two kinds:
  - **Busy families** near Wheaton College, with kids from elementary to high school.
  - **Older homeowners** who have lived in the area for years.
- Each quote has a note on where it came from:
  - **Luke:** Grad Gig's founder, describing the customer in his own words.
  - **Luke, what customers say:** the questions and complaints Luke says Grad Gig hears all the time.
  - **Tait:** our notes on the client.
- **Good to know:** none of this is from a call with a customer yet. "What customers say" is the closest we have to the buyer's own words. A recording of a real family or homeowner would make this even sharper.

## 3. The 5 phrases to use in hooks and headlines

These are already how the buyer talks: questions Luke says Grad Gig hears all the time, and the fears he names again and again.

1. **"I don't know who else to call."**
   *Why:* what customers say (Luke), and it matches the older homeowner's bad day: "they have nobody to call."
   *As a hook:* "Don't know who else to call? Call a local college student."
2. **"Is this job too small?"**
   *Why:* customers ask it (Luke), and it's one of the false beliefs he named: "this job is too small to hire somebody for."
   *As a hook:* "'Is this job too small?' Small jobs are exactly what we do."
3. **"I just need somebody I can trust in my house."**
   *Why:* what customers say (Luke), and the fear both kinds of customer share: "a stranger going into their home."
   *As a hook:* "You need somebody you can trust in your house. Here's how we check every student."
4. **"Are they actually Wheaton students?"**
   *Why:* the first question Luke lists. What Grad Gig can show: every student has a verified .edu email from an accredited college, activity is monitored, and every gig gets a rating (Grad Gig's FAQ). It does not run background checks.
   *As a hook:* "'Are they actually college students?' Here's how we check." (Say "Wheaton students" only once Luke confirms every student on the platform is from Wheaton.)
5. **"Can we have the same student back next time?"**
   *Why:* what customers say (Luke), and the dream: "friendly students who know their house."
   *As a hook:* "Yes, you can have the same student back next time."
